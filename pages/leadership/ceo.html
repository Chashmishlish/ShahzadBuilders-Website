INTRODUCTION OF

Faizan Qureshi

CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER (CEO)

Faizan Qureshi is the third-generation leader of a family whose name has been associated with trust, excellence, and innovation in Pakistan’s real estate industry for more than four decades. As the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Shahzad Builders & Developers, he proudly carries forward the legacy established by his grandfather, Muhammad Akhtar Qureshi, and further strengthened by his father, Shahzad Qureshi.

Following the passing of his father in 2021, Faizan Qureshi assumed the responsibility of leading the company with a clear vision for the future. Demonstrating resilience, determination, and strong leadership, the CEO successfully completed and delivered the project that was under construction at the time of his father’s passing, fulfilling every commitment made to clients while preserving the company’s reputation for quality, trust, and timely delivery.

Today, Shahzad Builders & Developers is led by Faizan Qureshi as Chief Executive Officer, alongside his brothers, Rizwan Qureshi and Salman Qureshi, who together continue to uphold the family’s vision and commitment to excellence.

Under the CEO’s leadership, the company has continued its journey of growth and excellence by successfully completing and delivering multiple residential and commercial projects. Every development reflects Shahzad Builders & Developers’ unwavering commitment to superior construction quality, modern design, timely execution, and complete customer satisfaction.

As an active member of the Association of Builders and Developers of Pakistan (ABAD), the CEO remains closely connected with Pakistan’s leading real estate and construction professionals. Through this association, he stays engaged with industry developments, promotes professional standards, and contributes to the continued growth and advancement of the country’s real estate sector.

With a forward-thinking approach and a passion for innovation, the CEO is committed to introducing modern construction practices, sustainable development, and contemporary living standards to Pakistan’s real estate sector. His vision extends beyond constructing buildings—he aims to create lasting value by building communities founded on trust, transparency, quality, and long-term relationships with clients and investors.

Today, the CEO continues to lead Shahzad Builders & Developers toward a future of sustainable growth and continued success. With a clear strategic vision, he is dedicated to expanding the company’s presence, delivering landmark developments, and upholding the values established by previous generations. His mission is to ensure that every project reflects the excellence, integrity, professionalism, and customer-focused approach that have defined the Qureshi family’s legacy for decades, while positioning the company among the most respected and trusted names in Pakistan’s real estate industry.
