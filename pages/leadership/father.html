INTRODUCTION OF

Shahzad Qureshi

CEO'S FATHER (LATE)

Following the passing of his father, Muhammad Akhtar Qureshi, Shahzad Qureshi took the responsibility of carrying forward his family's legacy with determination, vision, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. Building upon the strong foundation established by M.A. Estate, he founded Shahzad Builders & Developers, transforming the family business into one of Karachi's respected names in real estate development and construction.

Under his leadership, the company experienced remarkable growth, successfully delivering residential and commercial projects across Karachi's most prominent locations, including Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Clifton, DHA, Defence Housing Authority, North Nazimabad, North Karachi, and Gulistan-e-Jauhar. His dedication to quality construction, customer satisfaction, and ethical business practices earned him the trust of clients and investors alike.

Driven by an ambitious vision, Shahzad Qureshi expanded the company's operations beyond Pakistan, establishing a successful presence in Dubai, where he successfully undertook several real estate and business ventures. After gaining valuable international experience, he returned to Karachi in 2009 to continue contributing to the city's rapidly evolving real estate sector.

Upon his return, he led the development of several prestigious projects, including developments in Clifton, as well as upcoming projects on Jinnah Avenue, Gulistan-e-Jauhar, and in Shah Faisal Colony. His strategic vision, leadership, and commitment to innovation further strengthened the company's reputation and positioned it among the trusted names in the industry.

In 2021, Shahzad Qureshi passed away, leaving behind a remarkable legacy of excellence, integrity, and visionary leadership. Following his passing, his son, Faizan Qureshi, assumed the role of Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Shahzad Builders & Developers.

Carrying forward the values and vision established by his grandfather and father, Faizan Qureshi continues to lead the company into a new era of growth, innovation, and modern real estate development, ensuring that the legacy of the Qureshi family continues to thrive for generations to come.
